Our Commitment
At Hatelier, we believe in building inclusive, creative, and respectful spaces—online and offline. Our open-source initiatives are extensions of our studio’s values: trust, empathy, collaboration, and transparency.

We welcome contributors from all walks of life—whether you're an artist, developer, designer, educator, researcher, or enthusiast—your unique perspective is not only welcomed, it’s essential.

Our Pledge
In the interest of fostering an open and inspiring community, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of:

Age

Body size

Disability

Ethnicity

Gender identity and expression

Level of experience

Education

Socio-economic status

Nationality

Personal appearance

Race

Religion

Sexual identity and orientation

We are committed to creating an environment where everyone feels safe to share ideas, give and receive feedback, and grow together.

Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior include:

Using inclusive and respectful language

Being open to different perspectives

Giving and accepting constructive feedback with grace

Prioritizing collaboration over conflict

Acknowledging and amplifying the voices of underrepresented contributors

Supporting each other with kindness and professionalism

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

Violence, threats, or encouragement of self-harm

Discrimination, hate speech, or any form of bigotry

Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention

Trolling, gaslighting, or spreading misinformation

Harassment in any form (public or private)

Doxxing or sharing private information without consent

Using the reporting process to intimidate or silence others

Our Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying expectations of acceptable behavior and are empowered to take appropriate and fair corrective action if violations occur.

Maintainers reserve the right to remove or reject comments, commits, issues, wiki edits, and other contributions that don’t align with this Code of Conduct—and to temporarily or permanently ban any contributor whose behavior is inappropriate or harmful.

Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces—including GitHub discussions, pull requests, documentation comments, live collaboration sessions, community chats, and any events where you represent the project.

Enforcement
Unacceptable behavior can be reported—anonymously or directly—through our [Anonymous Reporting Form] or by contacting the project maintainers. All reports will be handled with confidentiality and care.

We prefer over-reporting to under-reporting. Even if you’re unsure, please speak up. You might help us improve not just the project, but the broader open-source ecosystem.

Project maintainers who fail to enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face review and removal from leadership roles.
